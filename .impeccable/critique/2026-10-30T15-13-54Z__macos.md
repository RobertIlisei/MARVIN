## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** a hybrid. The structure is category-standard: flat two-grey IDE shell, hairlines, native controls, system accent. A VS Code-adjacent app could ship it unchanged. The product-specific parts are the ones that carry the PRODUCT.md principles: Sessions ("Needs you / Working / Ready to integrate" with inline Allow/Deny and a conflict verdict), the confirm and decision cards, Review Changes, and the Practice loop. The mono voice for machine text gives the transcript a log-like character that fits. What is missing is a visible house style: the accent is the user's, the persona ("ultron / marvin") never reaches the chrome, and the light appearance loses the quiet entirely (U6).

**Deterministic scan:** not applicable (native platform).

## Overall Impression

The state model is excellent and the visual execution lags it. Sessions, confirms and Review Changes make the user overwatch real; the narrow-width panes, the status bar and the "reference-card" panes (Skills, Plugins, Practice, Backlog) undo that by spending the width on chrome, prose and repeated buttons. The single biggest opportunity: make the primary label win the row at every width, and move secondary actions behind hover, a menu or a detail view.

## What's Working

- **Sessions pane** (`pane-sessions--dark--420.png`): three state groups with counts, a spinner per working row, the Bash confirm answerable inline with its reason, and a per-branch conflict verdict ("1 would conflict, Sync first") before anything merges. This is Product Principle 3 delivered, and the render proves it.
- **AskUserQuestion sheet** (`chat-ask-question--light--560.png`): option cards with a one-line consequence each, "pick any" for multi-select, "Skip — you decide" as the exit, a disabled "Send choice" until there is one. Plain, specific copy.
- **Review Changes** (`review-changes--dark--820.png`): status glyphs (pencil/plus/minus), +/− counts, per-hunk Reject/Accept, real line numbers, split by default. The structure of the review is right; the weights on its buttons are not (U17).

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The status bar does not show its live values (U2).** At 700 pt every segment past the project name is an unlabelled icon; at 1200 pt the labels truncate ("ctx…", "grap…", "agen…", "$1.…", branch "f…er"). Context fill and cost, the two numbers an overwatch user needs, are cut off at both widths, while "Spaces: 4" is set visibly larger than its neighbours. Why: the bar costs 22 pt of every window and returns almost nothing. Fix: size segments to content with a priority order, collapse to value-only ("59%", "$1.84") rather than label-only, keep fonts uniform. Evidence: `status-bar--dark--700.png`, `status-bar--light--1200.png`. Command: `/impeccable layout`, then `/impeccable distill`.

**[P1] The scope-met marker prints as text (U1, your bug 2).** `<!-- marvin:scope-met -->` appears under every scope-met handoff and again the strip below says "Scope met". The detector (`ChatPreviewView.swift:3833-3837`) finds the sentinel, but the row renders the whole string; `ChatMessageRow.swift` has no strip step. Why: raw markup on the most frequent turn ending reads as a defect in the assistant. Fix: strip the sentinel from the rendered text, keep it in the transcript data. Evidence: `chat-pane--light--900.png` (y≈880), `chat-scope-met-handoff--dark--520.png`. Command: `/impeccable polish`.

**[P1] At narrow widths the primary label loses to secondary chrome, and several controls break outright (U3, U4).** Skills at 260: `migration-checklist` is `…`, others `api…view`, `slide…ilder`, while "Set source… / View" keep full width. Source Control at 260: file names truncate (`SyncService.s…`) while the directory path stays. Sessions at 260: "1 would conflict — Sync first" wraps one word per line; the title, branch and cost truncate. Plugins at 420: the Update chip is clipped ("Up"). Practice at 260: the tab strip loses "Runs", the "Fixed" chip is cut. Setup popover at 340: the VOICE control breaks "ultro/n", "marvi/n". Why: the left pane is meant to be dense; this is dense and unreadable. Fix: give the name the layout priority, move per-row secondary actions into a hover or context menu, and let controls wrap or collapse deliberately. Evidence: `pane-skills--light--260.png`, `pane-source-control--light--260.png`, `pane-sessions--light--260.png`, `pane-plugins--light--420.png`, `pane-practice--dark--260.png`, `topbar-setup-popover--dark--340.png`. Overlaps audit L17, L13. Command: `/impeccable adapt`, then `/impeccable layout`.

**[P1] Practice spends its first screen on boilerplate and model internals (U5).** At 260 the user scrolls past a four-line intro, two collapsed headers and two lines of fit statistics ("rank-correlation · 9/14 labelled · ρ 0.62") before the first finding; findings are titled with fingerprint ids (`ship…iewed`, `graph…kipped`) and a bare score ("0.74 / 1.00"). Why: the pane answers "what did MARVIN get wrong" and opens with how the ranking was fitted. Fix: lead with the pending proposals in plain language; move intro, schedule and fit into the "…" menu or a Details row. Evidence: `pane-practice--dark--260.png`, `pane-practice-rules--light--420.png`. Command: `/impeccable distill`, then `/impeccable clarify`.

**[P1] The light appearance leaves the Quiet Instrument (U6).** The editor tab strip and path bar, the terminal header, the diff column and hunk headers and the posture chip are a mid-grey (roughly #9f9f9f) against a white body, where DESIGN.md specifies `#F3F3F3` chrome separated by hairlines. Light syntax colours for types are pale cyan on white (not measured; visibly weak). The dark renders are calm; the light ones are heavy. Why: half the users run light, and the Hairline Rule is the system's identity. Fix: verify in the live app first (DESIGN.md "Not yet verified" lists this); then route these bands through `MarvinTheme.background`/`border` and give light its own syntax ramp. Evidence: `file-viewer--light--760.png`, `terminal--light--700.png`, `review-changes--light--1180.png`, `chat-pane--light--900.png`. Overlaps the audit's appearance finding and L15. Command: `/impeccable colorize`, then `/impeccable polish`.

**[P1, verify live] The dark terminal is unreadable (U7).** Fresh sessions in both widths draw a white background with default-white text; only coloured glyphs (the green ticks) show. It may be a capture artefact of the SwiftTerm view, so the severity is conditional. Evidence: `terminal--dark--700.png`, `terminal--dark--1000.png` (light is correct). Overlaps audit K26. Command: `/impeccable harden`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user, overseeing several tabs):** reads state at a glance in Sessions, then loses the thread in the status bar: cost and context are truncated at both widths, and a 5-button row repeats for every backlog item. "Prepare MR…" is styled as the primary action though it is the metered one.

**Sam (keyboard and low vision):** failure detail, paths and line:col are 9–10 pt tertiary; light-mode syntax types and added-diff text are pale on white; the graph is pointer-only; status segments are icon-only. Overlaps audit S1–S4.

**Overwatch Owen (project persona: the developer who stepped away for 40 minutes):** returns to a chat whose first screen is three chrome bars and a posture string he cannot decode (which model is the advisor? what is `auto`?), a handoff that ends in raw `<!-- marvin:scope-met -->`, and a Practice pane that opens with fit statistics instead of the one rule waiting for his approval.

## Minor Observations

- Section copy that cites ADRs ("ADR-0037", "ADR-0053/0054") belongs in docs, not in a pane header.
- Mono is used for pane names and chips as well as machine text; the Mono-For-Machines Rule is looser in the renders than in DESIGN.md.
- Files renders collapsed by default; nothing shows that rows expand until the chevrons are read.
- The Ask sheet gives no "1 of 2" for its second question below the fold.

## Questions to Consider

- What would the status bar be if it showed only three numbers (context, cost, branch) and everything else lived in a popover?
- If the posture row were one tappable sentence ("Opus 5 · advisor Sonnet 5 · Ultron · auto-approve"), would the chat need three bars above it?
- Should a pane's first screen ever be explanation, once the user has used it?
